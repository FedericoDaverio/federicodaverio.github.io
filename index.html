Federico Niccolò Daverio

Development economist researching how climate change, poverty and place shape the economic lives of households in Mexico.

I am a Financial Researcher at Banco de México, where I study how climate shocks such as droughts and hurricanes affect household credit and the stability of the financial system. My work combines large microdata, causal inference and machine learning to produce evidence that can inform policy.

I hold an MSc in Development Economics from CIDE, and a BSc and MSc in engineering and supply chain management from Politecnico di Milano. Before economics, I spent six years running the Mexican supply chain of an Italian design firm.
